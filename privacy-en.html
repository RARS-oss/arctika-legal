Privacy Policy — Arctika

Last revised: October 22, 2026

Arctika ("the App") is an offline guide to Crimea. This policy explains what data the App processes, where it is stored, and what leaves your device.

In short

  • The App has no registration and no account. Your profile, progress, favorites, and settings are stored only on your device. The App has no server of its own, and the developer does not receive this data.
  • The App does not request access to location, the camera, the microphone, contacts, or photos.
  • The only network request the App makes on its own is a weather request to the Open-Meteo service for the Crimean city you choose (see "Weather"). Like any web server, Open-Meteo sees your device's IP address.
  • No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics. Crash reporting is disabled in the current version.

Data stored on your device

  • Profile: your name or nickname, your age and your chosen companion (avatar).
  • Game progress: experience (XP), level, in-game distance, completed missions, "achievements", favorite places.
  • Settings: language, sound, vibration, appearance, weather city, and a record that you accepted the Terms of Use and this Policy.
  • Technical data: the last weather forecast received (cache) and technical in-app event counters. The counters are used only on the device and are not sent over the network.

This data is kept in the App's local database and settings and is not shared with the developer or any third party.

Weather

The App does not determine your location and does not use GPS. Weather is shown for the city you choose in Settings ("Weather region") from a fixed list of ten Crimean cities; the default is Yalta. Weather drives the screen ambience ("living sky") and "ideal conditions" hints for visiting places.

To get it, the App sends the coordinates of the chosen city's center (not yours) to the Open-Meteo service over a secure connection (HTTPS). Like any web server, Open-Meteo technically receives your device's IP address. The App does not send it your name, phone number, device identifiers, or any other information about you. The forecast received is stored on the device so it can be used offline.

Open-Meteo's own policy governs its processing of data: open-meteo.com/en/terms. Weather data is provided by Open-Meteo.com under the CC BY 4.0 license.

Phone sign-in (test builds only)

The public version of the App has no sign-in. Closed test (beta) builds may enable a phone-number sign-in screen with a test confirmation code. In that case:

  • the phone number is stored only on your device, in the system's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore);
  • the number is not sent anywhere and no SMS is sent; the confirmation code is a test code given to testers in advance;
  • on the first launch after installing or reinstalling, the App clears its secure storage, so sign-in data is removed together with the App.

Crash reports

Crash reporting is disabled in the current version. If we enable it in a future version, only technical information about a crash will be sent to Sentry (sentry.io): error type and message, stack trace, App and operating system version, device model. Your name, phone number, screenshots, and other personal data are not included. Like any web server, Sentry technically receives the device's IP address when a report is sent. We will update this Policy before enabling crash reports.

Voice narration

Narration of place stories uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine and works offline. The App passes the text only to that system engine and does not send it over the network; how the engine itself works is governed by your operating system settings.

Motion sensor

For a subtle depth (parallax) effect, the App reads the device accelerometer. These readings are processed on the fly, are not stored, and are not sent anywhere.

Permissions

The App does not request access to location, the camera, the microphone, contacts, or photos. Internet access is used only for the weather request and for opening external links you choose to follow. Place photos and texts are bundled with the App and are not downloaded from the network.

External links

The App contains links to third-party services: map apps (Yandex Maps, Google Maps, Apple Maps, 2GIS) for route planning, photo source and license pages, and a Telegram support chat. When you plan a route, only the place name is passed to the map app. Following a link takes you out of the App; those services are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Photos and place texts

Place photos come from Wikimedia Commons and Wikipedia and are used under free licenses (CC BY, CC BY-SA, public domain). Some place descriptions are based on Russian Wikipedia articles (CC BY-SA 4.0). Authors, licenses, and sources are listed on the "Photo sources" screen (Settings → Photo sources).

Data retention and deletion

  • Reset progress in Settings (level, XP, distance and achievements are reset).
  • Name, age and avatar can be changed in your profile.
  • All App data is removed when you uninstall the App.
  • On Android, backup of App data is disabled. On iOS, App data may be included in a device backup (iCloud or computer) that you create with system tools; the developer has no access to such backups.

Your rights

Because your data is stored only on your device, you control it yourself: you can view and change it in the App, reset your progress, or uninstall the App. For privacy questions, use the contact in the "Contact" section.

Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. The App does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available in the App with its revision date; we will announce material changes in an App update.
